OceanFit iOS App

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026 • Last Updated: October 16, 2026

Privacy First Summary: OceanFit stores 100% of your personal fitness profile, One-Rep Max (1RM) records, and workout logs locally on your iPhone. We do not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your personal data to external servers or third-party analytics providers.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To personalize your training programs, calculate barbell working weights, and track your workout streaks, OceanFit stores the following user-entered data locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage frameworks (UserDefaults and local app container storage):

  • Profile Information: First name, last name, optional contact fields, height, weight, fitness goal, and activity level.
  • Strength & Workout Records: One-Rep Max (1RM) values (Bench Press, Squat, Deadlift, Overhead Press), completed workout sets, session volume history, active streak count, and custom exercise swaps.
  • App Preferences: Preferred unit system (Imperial lbs/ft or Metric kg/cm), selected workout program, and onboarding completion state.

2. No Third-Party Tracking or Analytics

OceanFit does not embed any third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or cross-app tracking technologies. As declared in our Apple Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), NSPrivacyTracking is set to false.

3. Network Requests

OceanFit may fetch read-only workout program catalog definitions (workout1.json through workout5.json) over HTTPS from GitHub Pages (https://bretlindquist.github.io/OceanFit-Data/). These requests do not include any personal or device-identifying information, and the app functions completely offline using its built-in 20-program catalog.

4. Optional Trainer Email & Workout Sharing

  • Contacting a Trainer: If you tap "Schedule a Training Session" in your Profile, OceanFit opens Apple's native Mail sheet pre-populated with your chosen workout details. No email is sent unless you explicitly review and send it through your own email account.
  • Workout Share Cards: If you use the Share button to export a workout summary or Beach Workout Share Card, sharing is handled entirely by Apple's system share sheet (ShareLink) to destinations you choose.

5. Deleting Your Data

You have full control over your data at all times. You can permanently erase all saved profile data, 1RM records, and workout history immediately from within the app by opening Profile → Reset Profile & Clear All Data, or by uninstalling the OceanFit app from your iPhone.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or OceanFit's privacy practices, please visit our Support Page or contact us via GitHub at github.com/bretlindquist/Oceanfit_iOS.
