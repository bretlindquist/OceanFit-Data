Frequently Asked Questions

How does Automatic Linear Progression work?

Enter your starting 1-Rep Maxes (Bench Press, Squat, Deadlift, Overhead Press) during onboarding or via the 1RM & Plates quick action. OceanFit automatically calculates your working weights for every set. When you complete all prescribed sets on a primary compound lift, OceanFit bumps your 1RM by +5 lbs (Upper Body) or +10 lbs (Lower Body) on session completion.

How do I use the Ocean Pomodoro Wave Timer?

Select any of the 4 Beach & Calisthenics programs (such as Malibu Sand & Calisthenics Flow) or tap the Ocean Wave quick tool on the Today tab. Choose 20m Surge / 5m Breeze, 25m Surge / 5m Breeze, or 12m EMOM Wave and tap Start Wave.

Can I swap, add, or remove exercises in my workout?

Yes! Open any workout day and tap the ellipsis menu (•••) on an exercise card to swap it with a biomechanically matched movement or remove it, or tap the Add Exercise button in the top bar.

How do I reset my profile or clear my workout data?

Tap the Profile icon in the top-right corner, scroll to the bottom, and tap Reset Profile & Clear All Data. All data is stored locally on your iPhone.
